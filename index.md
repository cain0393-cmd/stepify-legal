Stepify – Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 21, 2026
Developer: Stepify
Contact: cain0393@gmail.com 
Stepify ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and deleted when you use the Stepify Android application (the "App").
1. Information We Collect
Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name (optional), and an encrypted authentication credential managed by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your plaintext password.
Guest Mode: If you use Stepify as a guest without creating an account, your preferences are stored locally on your device only.
Task & Progress Data: To provide core functionality, we store your momentum statistics (such as steps completed, tasks broken down, focus time, and active day streaks) in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your unique user ID.
AI Task Prompts: When you submit a task to be broken down into micro-steps, the text of that task is sent securely to Google Vertex AI (Gemini) to generate your step-by-step plan.
Local Preferences & Reminders: Settings such as your theme mode, haptic feedback preference, focus sprint duration, onboarding status, and daily reminder toggle are stored locally on your device. If you opt in to the Daily Gentle Nudge, the App schedules local notifications on your device using Android WorkManager; no notification data is sent to external servers.
Crash & Stability Diagnostics: In release versions of the App, we use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymized technical crash reports (such as stack traces, device model, and OS version) to fix bugs. We strictly exclude your task prompts, notes, emails, and personal information from crash logs.
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information described above solely to:
Authenticate your account and sync your momentum progress across sessions.
Generate micro-step task breakdowns using AI.
Deliver optional, user-enabled local daily reminders.
Diagnose crashes and maintain App stability.
We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your task data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
3. Third-Party Services
Stepify relies on trusted Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure to operate:
Firebase Authentication (account sign-in and credential security)
Google Cloud Firestore (cloud storage for user progress and stats)
Google Vertex AI / Gemini (AI-powered task decomposition)
Firebase Crashlytics (anonymized crash reporting)
Each service processes data in accordance with Google's privacy and security standards.
4. Data Security & Retention
Your account and progress data are protected in transit via HTTPS encryption and secured at rest using Google Cloud Firestore Security Rules, which restrict access so that only your authenticated account can read or write your personal records. We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active.
5. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)
You have full control over your data and can permanently delete your account at any time directly inside the App:
Open Stepify and go to the Profile tab.
Tap App Settings.
Scroll down and tap Delete Account & All Data, then confirm.
This action permanently deletes your personal document in Cloud Firestore, removes your Firebase Authentication profile, and clears your local session data. (For security, Firebase may require you to sign out and sign back in if your session is old.)
If you cannot access the App and need your account deleted, email [Your Support Email] from the email address associated with your account, and we will delete your data within 7 business days.
6. Not Medical or Clinical Advice
Stepify is a general productivity tool. It is not a medical device and does not collect clinical or protected health information (PHI). Do not enter sensitive medical, financial, or government identification details into task prompts.
7. Children's Privacy
Stepify is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at [Your Support Email] so we can promptly delete it.
